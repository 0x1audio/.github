## 0x1audio

We make **Mixel for Mac**, which helps you find the sound you're thinking of: describe it in words, and Mixel searches every sound in your folders, privately, on your Mac. Search in words needs a Mac with Apple silicon. [0x1audio.com](https://0x1audio.com)

### Build for Mixel

- **Apps:** a typed Python function that Mixel runs next to your Library, with a form it draws for you. Start with [Your first app](https://docs.0x1audio.com/start).
- **The kit:** [ox1audio](https://github.com/0x1audio/0x1audio-sdk-py) (the Python SDK and the `mixel` command) and [@ox1audio/sdk](https://github.com/0x1audio/0x1audio-sdk-ts) (pages and scripts in TypeScript).
- **The official apps,** open source under the Apache 2.0 licence: [Audio Converter](https://github.com/0x1audio/0x1audio-converter-app), [Vocal Extractor](https://github.com/0x1audio/0x1audio-uvr-app), [De-reverb](https://github.com/0x1audio/0x1audio-sonic-dryer-app) and [Pitch & Tempo](https://github.com/0x1audio/0x1audio-audiowizard-app). Vocal Extractor and De-reverb need Apple silicon.

Questions and things you've made: [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/0x1audio/discussions).
